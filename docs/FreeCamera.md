## Free Camera

A Free Camera feature has been added to the game. It allows you to take beautiful cinematic screenshots, and you can freely control the camera as well.

The camera is positioned near your character and is only visible to you. Other players cannot see it.

### Controls

Press **N** to enable Free Camera mode.

Use the **arrow keys** to change the camera's direction.

You can also adjust the camera's movement speed:

1. **Fast speed:** Press **6**.
2. **Slow speed:** Press **5**.
3. **Stop the camera:** Press **7**.

To return the camera to its original position, press **N** again.
